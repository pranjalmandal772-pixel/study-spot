# Business & Pricing Hypothesis

## Value proposition
StudySpot could reduce the small but repeated friction of finding an appropriate place to study.

## Initial customer
The initial user is a student. The MVP should be free because the first question is whether students find the recommendation useful at all.

## Possible future payer
If usage is validated, a university could be explored as a possible customer for a campus-specific version with space information and analytics. This would require interviews with university staff before treating it as a real business opportunity.

## Pricing hypothesis
Do not price the student MVP. If a university version were tested later, pricing should be based on the value of campus-wide deployment rather than charging individual students.

## Risks
- Students may already know their preferred study locations.
- Accurate live occupancy data may be difficult to obtain.
- Recommendations are only as useful as the location data.
- The problem may not be important enough to support a paid product.
